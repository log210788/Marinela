# 🍂 Marinela's Peaceful English Sanctuary & Lesson Notes System

A calm, peaceful interactive companion page for **Marinela's first English conversation lesson with Louie** (October 20, 2026), plus a reusable template and standalone demo for prospective students.

---

## 🌟 Interactive Pages in this Repository

1. **[Marinela's Lesson 01 Notes (index.html)](https://log210788.github.io/Marinela/)**:
   - The full, customized sanctuary created for Marinela's 29-minute lesson.
   - Includes all 76 cleaned turns, 13 speech-to-text spelling fixes, 58 flagged vocabulary words & idioms, and cultural deep-dives into Tadao Ando's *Church of the Light*, Winchester, and Bath.

2. **[Prospective Student Interactive Demo (demo.html)](https://log210788.github.io/Marinela/demo.html)**:
   - A standalone showcase file you can send directly to potential Preply / private students!
   - Demonstrates how your notes work, highlights the benefits of stress-free speaking without taking notes, showcases speech-to-text error clean-up, and includes flashcards, quiz, and a call-to-action button.

3. **[Template & Instructions for Future AI Models (template/)](./TEMPLATE_INSTRUCTIONS_FOR_AI.md)**:
   - Contains `template/index_template.html` and `template/GEMINI.md` to instantly spin up new student sites.
