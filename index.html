Vocabulary Spotlight 🕒 08:36

secondhand embarrassment

/ˌsek.əndˈhænd ɪmˈbær.əs.mənt/ noun phrase / idiom C1
Meaning & Nuance

Feeling acute embarrassment or cringe on behalf of another person who is behaving foolishly or breaking a social code without realizing it.

How It Was Used in Our Lesson

"It's secondhand embarrassment... You feel embarrassed for them because they don't realize."
