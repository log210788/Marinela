2. Winchester — England's Ancient Cathedral City

Just an hour southwest of London, Winchester was once the ancient capital of Anglo-Saxon England. With its medieval cathedral, historic college, and water-meadow walks (where the poet John Keats wrote "To Autumn"!), it feels remarkably like Oxford or a peaceful Renaissance hometown.

3. Bath — Golden Limestone & The River Avon

When the note-taker wrote "have you ever been to a bar at all?", Louie was actually asking: "Have you ever been to Bath at all?" Built from warm, honey-coloured oolitic limestone (Bath stone), its Georgian crescent facades catch the afternoon sun so that the light literally bathes Bath in a golden glow.

Louie's Description in the Lesson:

"It is truly magical. The river, the building facades—the fronts of the buildings—they're absolutely lovely... It gives the city a magical glow. It's like the light bathes Bath in a lovely way."
